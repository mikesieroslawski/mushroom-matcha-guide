## What's actually been studied

Green tea catechins, particularly EGCG, combined with caffeine, have been studied for a modest effect on metabolic rate and fat oxidation in some clinical research. The effect sizes in these studies are generally small, and results vary by study design, population, and dose.

## What has not been studied

There is no clinical research specifically on mushroom matcha, or on Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, or Reishi, for weight loss. Any weight-loss claim for this specific blend would be extrapolating from general green tea research, not evidence about the product itself.

## The honest framing

Mushroom matcha may reasonably fit into a routine focused on weight management the way any lower-calorie beverage swapped in for a higher-calorie one might, replacing a sugary drink, for instance, but that's a general dietary-substitution effect, not a specific benefit of the mushroom matcha blend.
