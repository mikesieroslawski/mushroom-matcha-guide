title: "Does Mushroom Matcha Break a Fast?"
description: "Mushroom matcha has minimal calories on its own, but whether it technically breaks a fast depends on the fasting protocol and any added milk or sweetener."
---

## Plain mushroom matcha, whisked with water only

A serving of mushroom matcha powder whisked into water alone has minimal calories, typically in the low single digits per gram of powder. Under most intermittent fasting protocols, a very low-calorie drink like this is generally considered acceptable, though strict water-only fasting protocols would not permit it.

## What actually breaks a fast

Adding milk (dairy or plant-based) or sweetener introduces meaningful calories and, in the case of dairy or many plant milks, protein and sugar that can trigger a metabolic response some fasting protocols are designed to avoid. A mushroom matcha latte with milk is a different consideration than plain mushroom matcha with water.

## What about the mushroom powder itself

Whole mushroom powder in the small quantities used per serving (roughly 1g total blended powder) contributes negligible calories on its own.
