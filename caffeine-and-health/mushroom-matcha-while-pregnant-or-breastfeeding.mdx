## The caffeine consideration

Major health guidance generally recommends limiting caffeine intake during pregnancy, commonly cited around 200mg per day as a widely referenced ceiling, though guidance varies by source and should come from a doctor rather than a general guide. At 30-70mg per cup, mushroom matcha's caffeine needs to be counted against that daily total alongside coffee, other tea, and any other caffeinated food or drink.

## The mushroom consideration

Clinical safety data for Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi specifically during pregnancy or breastfeeding is limited. Traditional use doesn't equal established safety in pregnancy, since many traditional-use ingredients haven't been specifically studied in pregnant or breastfeeding populations using modern clinical standards.

## The bottom line

This is a conversation for a doctor or midwife, not a general content guide. Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding should discuss both the caffeine content and the mushroom ingredients with a healthcare provider before making mushroom matcha a regular habit.
