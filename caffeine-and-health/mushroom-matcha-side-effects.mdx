## The most likely side effect: caffeine sensitivity

Because mushroom matcha is genuinely caffeinated (30-70mg per cup, see [Mushroom Matcha Caffeine Content](/caffeine-and-health/mushroom-matcha-caffeine-content)), the most common side effects mirror any caffeinated drink: jitteriness in caffeine-sensitive people, disrupted sleep if consumed too late in the day, and mild stomach upset on an empty stomach for some people.

## Less common considerations

- **Mushroom allergies.** Though uncommon, anyone with a known mushroom allergy should avoid mushroom-blended products, tea-based or not.
- **Medication interactions.** Reishi in particular has been studied for interaction with blood-thinning medication in a small number of case reports; anyone on blood thinners or other prescription medication should check with a doctor before regular use.
- **Digestive changes.** Some people report mild digestive changes when introducing any new fiber- or compound-rich powder to their diet, mushroom-based or not; this typically resolves as the body adjusts.

## What this page is not

Not a substitute for medical advice. Anyone with an existing medical condition, taking prescription medication, pregnant, or breastfeeding should talk to a doctor before making mushroom matcha a daily habit.
