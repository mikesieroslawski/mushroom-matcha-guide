## The caffeine angle

Caffeine, matcha's primary active compound relevant here, is one of the more well-studied ergogenic (performance-enhancing) substances in sports science, with research supporting modest benefits to endurance and perceived exertion at moderate doses. At 30-70mg per cup, mushroom matcha's caffeine content is real but generally more moderate than a dedicated pre-workout supplement.

## The Cordyceps angle

Some studies, largely in athletes or older adults, have examined Cordyceps' effect on exercise performance markers like oxygen utilization. Results are mixed rather than uniformly positive across these studies, and sample sizes are often small.

## What hasn't been studied

No research has tested mushroom matcha as a combined product for exercise performance specifically. The component research above (caffeine, Cordyceps) is suggestive, not proof of a specific effect from this blend.

## A practical note on timing

If using mushroom matcha before exercise, the same general caffeine-timing logic applies as with coffee, roughly 30-60 minutes before activity is a commonly cited window for caffeine's effects to take hold.
