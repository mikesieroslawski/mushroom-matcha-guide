## What's studied on the tea side

L-theanine, an amino acid naturally present in matcha, has been studied in human trials for its relationship to relaxation and stress response, often in combination with caffeine, with some research showing a calmer, steadier alertness profile than caffeine alone.

## What's studied on the mushroom side

Reishi is traditionally used for calming and grounding qualities, and a 2026 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Hisamuddin and colleagues, published in Brain and Behavior) tested a mushroom blend containing Reishi in 50 participants over 12 weeks, finding reductions in cortisol, ACTH, and CRP alongside improved anxiety, fatigue, and sleep quality scores. That study used a specific mushroom blend, not necessarily identical in dose or composition to any one commercial product.

## What hasn't been studied

No clinical research has tested mushroom matcha as a combined product for cortisol or stress outcomes specifically. The individual-ingredient research above is suggestive, not proof of an effect from this exact blend.
