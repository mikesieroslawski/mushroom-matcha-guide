## The tea's role: caffeine plus L-theanine

The combination of caffeine and L-theanine, both naturally present in matcha, has been studied in human trials for cognitive performance, with some research suggesting the pairing produces steadier, calmer alertness than caffeine alone, an effect sometimes described as reduced jitteriness alongside maintained focus.

## The mushroom's role: Lion's Mane

A small number of human studies have examined Lion's Mane and cognitive function, generally in specific populations such as older adults with mild cognitive concerns, with some showing modest improvement on standardized tests during supplementation. This is an active but limited research area, and results haven't consistently replicated at scale.

## Putting the two together

Mushroom matcha's focus claim rests on two separate bodies of research, tea-based (caffeine plus L-theanine, more established) and mushroom-based (Lion's Mane, more preliminary), not on a single study of the combined product.
