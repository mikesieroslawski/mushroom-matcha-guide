## What hasn't been studied

There is no clinical research specifically testing mushroom matcha, or its combination of ceremonial matcha with Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi, for liver or kidney health outcomes. This page does not claim a benefit or a risk that isn't supported by evidence.

## What's worth knowing generally

Green tea in very high concentrated-extract doses has, in rare cases, been associated with liver concerns in case reports, generally involving concentrated supplement-form extracts rather than brewed or whisked tea consumed at typical amounts. Standard matcha consumption at normal serving sizes is a different exposure level than concentrated extract supplements.

## Why this matters for anyone with existing conditions

Anyone with an existing liver or kidney condition, or taking medication that affects either organ, should talk to a doctor before making any new tea, supplement, or mushroom-containing product a regular daily habit, since individual medical circumstances vary widely.
