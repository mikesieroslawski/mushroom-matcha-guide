## What Cordyceps is

Cordyceps is a genus of fungi long used in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Most commercial Cordyceps products today, including cultivated strains like Cordyceps militaris or lab-cultivated Cordyceps sinensis, are grown rather than wild-harvested, a meaningful difference from the historically rare wild fungus.

## The traditional association

Historically used in Traditional Chinese Medicine for stamina, recovery, and general vitality.

## What modern research actually shows

Some studies, largely conducted in athletes or older adults, have examined Cordyceps' effect on exercise performance markers like oxygen utilization (VO2 max and related metabolic thresholds). Results across these studies are mixed rather than uniformly positive, and many rely on small sample sizes, worth keeping in mind when weighing the traditional stamina association against the modern evidence.

## An important sourcing note

Different Cordyceps products use different species and processing methods (fruiting body vs mycelium, wild vs cultivated), which can affect both the traditional framing and the specific research that applies. Check what species and form a specific product actually uses rather than assuming all "Cordyceps" products are identical.
