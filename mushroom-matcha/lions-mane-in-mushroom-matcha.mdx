## What Lion's Mane is

Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus) is an edible mushroom traditionally used in East Asian cooking and herbal practice, recognizable by its distinctive white, shaggy, icicle-like appearance when fresh.

## The traditional association

Traditionally linked to mental clarity and focus in East Asian herbal practice, long before modern research began examining it.

## What modern research actually shows

A small number of human studies have examined Lion's Mane and cognitive function, generally in specific populations such as older adults with mild cognitive concerns. Some have shown modest improvement on standardized cognitive tests during the supplementation period. This is an active but limited research area, not a settled clinical finding, and results haven't consistently replicated at scale across larger populations.

## What this page does not claim

Lion's Mane, in mushroom matcha or any other product, does not treat, cure, or prevent any disease or cognitive condition. That's a medical claim this page, and the FDA's structure/function rules for food and supplement products, do not permit.
