<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is mushroom matcha a mushroom?">
    No. It's matcha (tea) blended with mushroom powder. The base is tea, not fungus.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does mushroom matcha have caffeine?">
    Yes, commonly cited at 30-70mg per 8oz cup, a genuinely caffeinated drink, not a low-caffeine one. See [Mushroom Matcha Caffeine Content](/caffeine-and-health/mushroom-matcha-caffeine-content).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does mushroom matcha taste like mushrooms?">
    Not strongly; matcha's grassy, umami flavor dominates. See [Does Mushroom Matcha Actually Taste Like Mushrooms?](/mushroom-matcha/does-mushroom-matcha-taste-like-mushrooms)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is mushroom matcha different from plain matcha?">
    Whole Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi mushroom powder is blended into the tea; everything else (caffeine, flavor base, brewing method) stays essentially the same. See [Mushroom Matcha vs Plain Matcha](/comparisons/mushroom-matcha-vs-matcha).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is mushroom matcha the same as mushroom coffee?">
    No, different base drink entirely. See [Mushroom Matcha vs Mushroom Coffee](/comparisons/mushroom-matcha-vs-mushroom-coffee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make mushroom matcha?">
    Whisk about 1g of powder into 2oz of warm water (140-175°F) in a zigzag motion until smooth. See [How to Make Mushroom Matcha](/brewing/how-to-make-mushroom-matcha).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is mushroom matcha safe?">
    Generally yes for healthy adults in moderate amounts, with caution for caffeine sensitivity, pregnancy, mushroom allergies, and blood-thinning medication. See [Is Mushroom Matcha Safe?](/mushroom-matcha/is-mushroom-matcha-safe)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
