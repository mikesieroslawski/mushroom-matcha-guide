## The dosing difference

Mushroom supplement capsules are typically formulated to deliver a specific, concentrated dose of one or more mushroom extracts or powders, often standardized to a particular compound. Mushroom matcha delivers a comparatively smaller amount of whole mushroom powder, blended into a much larger volume of tea, prioritizing an everyday drinkable habit over a precise supplement dose.

## The experience difference

Capsules are swallowed with water in seconds, no flavor, no ritual. Mushroom matcha is whisked and consumed as a beverage, with matcha's flavor and a few minutes of preparation built into the experience, a meaningfully different daily habit even when the underlying mushroom ingredients overlap.

## Which one fits which goal

If the goal is a precise, concentrated mushroom dose independent of any tea or caffeine, capsules are the more direct fit. If the goal is working mushroom ingredients into an already-enjoyed tea ritual, with matcha's flavor and caffeine as part of the appeal rather than incidental, mushroom matcha fits better.
