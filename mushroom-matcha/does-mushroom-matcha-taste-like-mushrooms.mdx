title: "Does Mushroom Matcha Actually Taste Like Mushrooms?"
description: "Not really. Matcha's grassy, umami flavor dominates; the mushroom powder mostly contributes body and a faint earthiness."
---

## The direct answer

No, not strongly. In a well-formulated mushroom matcha, the ceremonial matcha makes up the large majority of the blend by both weight and flavor impact. The whole mushroom powder (Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi) is present in a small enough quantity that it doesn't read as a distinct "mushroom" flavor the way, say, a mushroom broth or sautéed mushrooms would.

## What the mushroom powder does contribute

A subtle earthiness and a slightly thicker, more substantial mouthfeel compared to plain matcha. Some people notice this; others don't perceive a difference from plain matcha at all.

## When mushroom flavor becomes more noticeable

If a product uses a higher ratio of mushroom powder to matcha than typical, or lower-quality culinary-grade matcha that has less flavor of its own to dominate the cup, the mushroom notes can become more apparent by comparison.
