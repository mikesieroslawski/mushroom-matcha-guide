## The complete ingredient list

A clean-label mushroom matcha contains exactly four things: ceremonial-grade matcha (stone-milled Japanese green tea), Lion's Mane mushroom powder, Cordyceps mushroom powder, and Reishi mushroom powder. No fillers, no added sugar, no artificial flavoring.

## What's not in it, on a well-made product

- No bulking starches (rice flour, maltodextrin)
- No artificial sweeteners or flavoring
- No proprietary "mushroom blend" hiding which specific mushrooms or how much of each
- No mystery ingredients beyond the four named above

## How to verify a specific product's ingredients

Check the label for: matcha grade stated (ceremonial vs culinary), each mushroom named individually (not a vague "mushroom blend"), and whole powder vs extract disclosed. See [Mushroom Matcha Ingredients](/basics/mushroom-matcha-ingredients) for the full checklist.
