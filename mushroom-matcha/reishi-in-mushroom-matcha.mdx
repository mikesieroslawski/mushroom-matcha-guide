## What Reishi is

Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum and related species) is a mushroom with a long history in East Asian herbal traditions, often called the "mushroom of balance" or "mushroom of immortality" in traditional contexts.

## The traditional association

Traditionally used for calming, grounding, and general wellness purposes, among the most storied mushrooms in East Asian herbal practice.

## What modern research actually shows

Reishi contains triterpene compounds studied for their interaction with the immune system, primarily in animal and in-vitro research, with more limited human research. A 2026 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Hisamuddin and colleagues, published in Brain and Behavior) tested a mushroom blend containing Reishi in 50 participants over 12 weeks, finding reductions in cortisol, ACTH, and CRP alongside improved anxiety, fatigue, and sleep quality scores, a promising but single-study result using a specific blend, not proof of an isolated Reishi effect.

## What this page does not claim

Reishi is not a treatment for any medical condition, anxiety disorder, or sleep disorder. Traditional-use and preliminary-research framing applies throughout.
