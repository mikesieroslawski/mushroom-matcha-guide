## For most healthy adults

Mushroom matcha, taken in moderate daily amounts (one to two servings), is generally considered reasonable for healthy adults, combining a long-established food (matcha) with mushroom ingredients that have extensive traditional-use history, though not the same depth of modern clinical safety research as, say, a pharmaceutical.

## Who should be more cautious

- **Anyone caffeine-sensitive.** At 30-70mg per cup, this is real caffeine, not a caffeine-free option.
- **Pregnant or breastfeeding people.** See [Mushroom Matcha While Pregnant or Breastfeeding](/caffeine-and-health/mushroom-matcha-while-pregnant-or-breastfeeding).
- **Anyone with a mushroom allergy.** Though uncommon, this is a real contraindication.
- **Anyone on blood-thinning medication.** Reishi in particular has limited case-report-level research on interaction with anticoagulants.
- **Anyone with an existing medical condition**, who should check with a doctor before adding any new supplement-adjacent ingredient to a daily routine.

## What "safe" does not mean here

Safe for most healthy adults in moderate amounts is not the same as clinically proven to treat or prevent any condition. This page makes no such claim.
