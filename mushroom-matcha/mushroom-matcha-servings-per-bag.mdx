## The typical math

A 1oz (28g) bag of mushroom matcha, at a stated serving size of roughly 1g of powder per cup, works out to about 28 servings per bag, close to a month of daily single cups.

## Why the serving size is smaller than it might sound

Matcha is potent relative to its weight; 1g whisked into 2oz of water and topped with milk produces a full 8oz drink with real flavor and caffeine, unlike, say, coffee grounds which are typically used in much larger per-serving quantities.

## What affects how far a bag actually goes

- **Serving strength preference.** Some people use slightly more or less than 1g depending on how strong they like their matcha.
- **Latte vs straight tea.** Both use the same amount of powder; the difference is in added milk volume, not powder amount.
- **Multiple servings per day.** Two cups daily roughly halves how long a single bag lasts compared to one cup a day.
