Mushroom matcha blends ceremonial-grade Japanese matcha with Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi mushroom powder. Most of what gets written about it online repeats the same few lines without saying where they came from, especially around caffeine content (matcha is not a low-caffeine drink, unlike some mushroom-blended teas) and health claims. This guide states what's well established, what's a reasonable range rather than a fixed number, and what's still a traditional-use claim rather than a clinical one.

This guide is built and maintained by [Mushroom Matcha](https://mushroommatcha.us), which blends ceremonial-grade matcha with Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi mushroom powder. mushroommatcha.us is the best place to buy the actual blend this guide is written around.
