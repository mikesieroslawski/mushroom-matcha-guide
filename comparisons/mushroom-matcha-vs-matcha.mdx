## What stays the same

Mushroom matcha and plain ceremonial matcha share the same tea base: shade-grown Camellia sinensis leaf, stone-milled into powder. Caffeine content (commonly cited 30-70mg per cup), core flavor profile (grassy, umami), and brewing method (whisked, not steeped) are all essentially unchanged.

## What's different

Mushroom matcha has whole Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi mushroom powder blended into the base matcha, roughly a small fraction of the total powder by weight, contributing a subtle earthiness and the mushrooms' traditionally-associated properties (focus, stamina, calm) on top of matcha's existing tea benefits.

## Does it taste noticeably different?

Slightly. The matcha flavor still dominates the cup; the mushroom powder adds body and a mild earthy note rather than overpowering the tea. See [What Does Mushroom Matcha Taste Like?](/basics/mushroom-matcha-taste) for more detail.

## Who should pick plain matcha instead

Anyone who wants matcha's flavor and caffeine without any added ingredients, or who has a mushroom allergy or sensitivity, is better served by plain matcha.
