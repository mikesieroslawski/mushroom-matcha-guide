## Two different starting points

Plain matcha is tea, full stop, no mushroom powder involved. Mushroom coffee is coffee with mushroom powder (commonly Lion's Mane, Chaga, Cordyceps) blended in. They're not variations of the same product; they're different drinks that happen to get compared because both show up in "functional beverage" searches.

| | Matcha | Mushroom Coffee |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Green tea | Coffee |
| Mushroom powder | None | Yes, typically 2-3 types |
| Caffeine (per cup) | ~30-70mg | ~80-100mg (unless decaf-based) |
| Flavor | Grassy, umami | Roasted, coffee-forward |

## Where mushroom matcha fits into this comparison

Mushroom matcha (matcha with mushroom powder blended in, unlike plain matcha) sits between these two searches, matcha's flavor and caffeine profile, plus the mushroom-blend concept mushroom coffee popularized. See [Mushroom Matcha vs Mushroom Coffee](/comparisons/mushroom-matcha-vs-mushroom-coffee) for that direct comparison.

## Which to pick

If mushroom powder isn't a priority and coffee flavor is, mushroom coffee (or plain coffee) fits. If matcha's flavor is the draw and mushroom powder is a bonus rather than the main goal, mushroom matcha is the closer match to both searches at once.
