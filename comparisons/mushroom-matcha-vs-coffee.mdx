## Caffeine

Coffee is commonly cited in the 80-100mg range per 8oz cup. Mushroom matcha is commonly cited lower, roughly 30-70mg, real caffeine, just generally less of it than a cup of coffee.

## Taste

Coffee's flavor is roasted, often bitter or acidic. Mushroom matcha's flavor is grassy, umami-forward, and naturally a little sweet if brewed correctly, a genuinely different flavor category, not a coffee substitute in the way a roasted tea like hojicha can be.

| | Coffee | Mushroom Matcha |
|---|---|---|
| Caffeine (per cup) | ~80-100mg | ~30-70mg |
| Flavor | Roasted, bitter, acidic | Grassy, umami, mildly sweet |
| Alertness profile | Caffeine alone | Caffeine plus L-theanine |

## What switching actually feels like

People switching from coffee to mushroom matcha generally notice two things: the flavor is a bigger adjustment than switching to a roasted tea would be, since matcha doesn't taste anything like coffee, and the alertness, thanks to L-theanine, is often described as calmer and less jittery even though real caffeine is still involved.
