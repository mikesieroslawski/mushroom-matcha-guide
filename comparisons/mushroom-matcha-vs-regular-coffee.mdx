## It's not a like-for-like swap

Unlike a roasted tea that can mimic coffee's flavor, mushroom matcha tastes nothing like coffee, grassy and umami rather than roasted and bitter. Anyone expecting a coffee-flavored substitute will likely be surprised; anyone open to a genuinely different morning drink has more room to appreciate it on its own terms.

## Caffeine: real, but generally less

Regular coffee is commonly cited at 80-100mg per cup; mushroom matcha at 30-70mg. Both are real caffeine sources, coffee typically delivers more per cup.

## The ritual difference

Coffee brewing (drip, pour-over, espresso) and matcha whisking are different physical rituals. Some people find the few minutes of whisking matcha into a smooth concentrate a meditative morning habit in its own right, distinct from, not a faster version of, a coffee routine.

## A realistic way to try switching

Rather than swapping cold turkey, some people start by replacing an afternoon coffee with mushroom matcha first, keeping morning coffee intact, to get used to the flavor before considering a full switch.
