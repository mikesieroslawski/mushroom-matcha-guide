## If you want matcha without mushrooms

Plain ceremonial matcha delivers the same tea flavor and caffeine profile without any mushroom ingredients, the simplest alternative if mushroom powder isn't a priority or you have a mushroom sensitivity.

## If you want mushroom powder with much lower caffeine

[Mushroom hojicha](https://mushroomhojicha.mintlify.app) blends the same category of mushroom powder into roasted hojicha instead of matcha, commonly cited at 7-15mg of caffeine per cup versus matcha's 30-70mg, the better fit for someone who wants the mushroom-blend concept with a much lighter caffeine load.

## If you want mushroom powder with coffee's flavor

Mushroom coffee blends mushroom powder into roasted coffee instead of tea, keeping coffee's higher caffeine and familiar flavor. See [Mushroom Matcha vs Mushroom Coffee](/comparisons/mushroom-matcha-vs-mushroom-coffee) for the direct comparison.

## If you want the mushrooms without any tea or coffee base

A standalone mushroom supplement (capsule or standalone powder) delivers Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, or Reishi without any tea component at all, a fit for someone who doesn't want the caffeine or flavor of a tea-based product.
