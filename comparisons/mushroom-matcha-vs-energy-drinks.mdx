## Caffeine content

Many commercial energy drinks contain 80-300mg of caffeine per serving, often alongside other stimulants like taurine or guarana extract. Mushroom matcha's commonly cited 30-70mg per cup is generally lower and comes from a single natural source (tea) rather than a stacked stimulant blend.

## Ingredients

Energy drinks frequently include added sugar (or artificial sweeteners), synthetic vitamins, and stimulant blends beyond caffeine alone. A clean-label mushroom matcha has four ingredients: ceremonial matcha, Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi mushroom powder, no added sugar or synthetic additives in its plain form.

## Alertness profile

Energy drinks' stimulant stacking is generally associated with a sharper onset and, for some people, a more pronounced crash. Matcha's L-theanine-caffeine combination is more often described as a steadier, gradual alertness without the same crash pattern, though individual response varies.
