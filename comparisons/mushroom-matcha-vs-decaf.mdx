## The key distinction

Decaf coffee has had most of its caffeine removed through a decaffeination process, typically retaining only a small residual amount (commonly a few mg per cup). Mushroom matcha is not decaffeinated at all, it's commonly cited at 30-70mg of caffeine per 8oz cup, a genuinely caffeinated drink.

## If the goal is avoiding caffeine

Decaf coffee is the more direct fit if the actual goal is minimizing caffeine intake. Mushroom matcha, despite sometimes appearing in "healthy coffee alternative" searches alongside decaf, is not a low-caffeine or caffeine-free option.

## If the goal is a different kind of alertness

If the goal isn't avoiding caffeine but wanting a steadier, less jittery form of it, mushroom matcha's L-theanine-caffeine combination is a genuinely different profile than either regular or decaf coffee offers.
