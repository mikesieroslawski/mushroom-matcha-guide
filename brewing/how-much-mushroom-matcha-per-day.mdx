## The typical range

One to two 8oz servings per day, at roughly 1g of powder each, is a common starting range, similar to how people typically approach plain matcha or green tea. There's no official maximum specific to mushroom matcha.

## What actually limits intake: caffeine

At 30-70mg of caffeine per cup, two servings could put total caffeine intake in the 60-140mg range, worth counting alongside coffee, other tea, or any other caffeinated food or drink consumed the same day, particularly for anyone sensitive to caffeine or managing a medical condition that requires caffeine limits.

## Is there an upper limit for the mushroom ingredients?

Specific upper-limit research for daily Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, or Reishi intake from a blended tea product like this is limited. Traditional use doesn't equal a clinically established maximum safe dose. Anyone wanting mushroom-specific dosing guidance should talk to a doctor rather than relying on general habit.
