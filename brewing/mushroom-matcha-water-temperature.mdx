## The commonly cited range

140-175°F (roughly 60-80°C) is the commonly cited target range for whisking matcha, mushroom-blended or plain. That's meaningfully below a full boil (212°F).

## What happens with water that's too hot

Water at or near boiling can scorch matcha's delicate compounds, pulling out excess bitterness and dulling the vivid green color and grassy, umami flavor that good matcha should have. This applies to mushroom matcha exactly as it does to plain matcha, the mushroom powder doesn't change the tea's heat sensitivity.

## A simple way to hit the range without a thermometer

Bring water to a boil, then let it sit off heat for 2 to 4 minutes before pouring; that generally lands the water somewhere in the target range without needing a temperature-controlled kettle.
