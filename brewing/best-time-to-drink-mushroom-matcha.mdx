## The general rule

Since mushroom matcha is genuinely caffeinated (30-70mg per cup), the same timing logic that applies to coffee applies here: morning through early-to-mid afternoon is generally safer for sleep than evening, since caffeine's half-life in the body is typically several hours.

## Why people reach for it in the morning

The combination of caffeine and L-theanine is often described as producing steadier, calmer alertness than coffee's caffeine alone, which is part of why it's frequently positioned as a morning replacement for coffee rather than an evening wind-down drink.

## What about an afternoon cup

An early-afternoon cup is reasonable for people without high caffeine sensitivity, but anyone who notices sleep disruption from afternoon coffee should apply the same caution to mushroom matcha, since the caffeine source doesn't change how the body processes it.
