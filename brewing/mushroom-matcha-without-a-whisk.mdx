## The frother method

A small handheld or electric milk frother is the closest substitute to a bamboo whisk for most people, producing a reasonably smooth result with the same zigzag-style agitation principle, just motorized.

## The shaker jar method

Add the powder and a small amount of warm water to a jar with a tight-fitting lid, and shake vigorously for 20-30 seconds. This won't produce quite the same fine foam as whisking, but it does suspend the powder reasonably well and works with equipment most kitchens already have.

## The spoon method

Vigorous stirring with a regular spoon, in a fast back-and-forth or figure-eight motion rather than slow circles, is the least effective of the three but can work if nothing else is available, expect more visible clumping.

## Which method to pick

If a smooth, latte-shop-quality result matters, a frother is the best non-whisk option. If convenience matters more, the shaker jar method requires no special tool at all.
