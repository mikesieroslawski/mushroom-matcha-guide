## What you need

- Mushroom matcha powder (roughly ½ to 1 teaspoon, about 1g, per serving)
- Warm water, 140-175°F, not boiling
- A bamboo whisk (chasen), small frother, or a spoon in a pinch
- A wide, shallow bowl or cup for whisking

## Step-by-step

1. **Sift the powder if clumpy.** A small sieve breaks up clumps for a smoother whisk, optional but helpful.
2. **Add powder to the bowl or cup.**
3. **Pour in about 2oz of warm water.**
4. **Whisk briskly in a zigzag (W or M) motion** until smooth and lightly foamy, roughly 15-20 seconds. Circular stirring won't produce the same smooth suspension.
5. **Drink as-is**, or top with more hot or cold water, or milk, for a larger or latte-style drink.

## Why the zigzag motion matters

A zigzag or "W" whisking motion incorporates air more effectively than circular stirring, which is what produces matcha's characteristic light foam and helps fully suspend the powder rather than leaving it clumped at the bottom.
