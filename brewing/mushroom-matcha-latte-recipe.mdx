## Hot mushroom matcha latte

**Ingredients**
- 1g (about ½-1 tsp) mushroom matcha powder
- 2oz hot water (140-175°F, not boiling)
- 6oz milk (dairy or a neutral plant milk), steamed or heated
- Sweetener, optional

**Method**
1. Whisk the powder into the hot water until smooth, no clumps remaining.
2. Steam or heat the milk separately.
3. Pour the steamed milk over the matcha concentrate, or combine and froth together.
4. Sweeten to taste if desired.

## Iced mushroom matcha latte

**Ingredients**
- 1g mushroom matcha powder
- 2oz hot water, to dissolve the powder
- 6oz cold milk
- Ice

**Method**
1. Whisk the powder into hot water first, exactly as in the hot version, cold water alone won't dissolve it cleanly.
2. Fill a glass with ice.
3. Pour in the cold milk.
4. Pour the matcha concentrate over the top and stir.

## Why the hot-water step matters even for iced

Matcha powder doesn't dissolve cleanly in cold liquid without first being worked into a smooth concentrate in warm water. Skipping this step is the most common reason for a clumpy, gritty iced latte.
