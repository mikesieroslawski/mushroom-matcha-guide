## What the powder is

Mushroom matcha powder is two things stone-milled or blended together: ceremonial-grade Japanese matcha (shade-grown tea leaf, ground fine) and whole mushroom powder from Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi. It is not a mushroom supplement with matcha flavoring added, and it is not plain matcha with a mushroom extract dropped in, the tea is the base, and the mushroom powder is blended into it.

## Whole powder vs extract

Whole mushroom powder means the dried, ground fruiting body (and in some products, mycelium) is used directly, as opposed to a concentrated extract standardized to one active compound. Extracts can deliver a more concentrated dose of a specific compound; whole powder preserves the full range of what's naturally in the mushroom, at a lower per-gram concentration.

## What to check on a label

- **Matcha grade stated.** Ceremonial grade (first-harvest, shade-grown) vs culinary grade is a meaningful quality difference.
- **Whole powder vs extract disclosed**, and if extract, what it's standardized to.
- **Each mushroom named specifically**, not just a generic "mushroom blend."
