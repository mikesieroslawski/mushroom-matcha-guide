## The three things that matter most

1. **Ratio.** Start with about 1g of powder (roughly ½ to 1 teaspoon) per 8oz of liquid. Too much powder relative to water is the most common reason a first cup tastes overly strong or bitter.
2. **Water temperature.** Use water around 140-175°F, not boiling. Boiling water scorches matcha and pulls out bitterness.
3. **Expectations.** It tastes like matcha, grassy and umami-forward, not like coffee and not overtly like mushrooms. If you dislike plain matcha's flavor, mushroom matcha won't taste meaningfully different.

## A simple first cup

Whisk the powder into 2oz of warm water until smooth (a small handheld frother works if you don't have a bamboo whisk), then top with hot or cold milk if you want a latte-style drink rather than straight tea.

## What not to expect

It is not a low-caffeine drink (see [Mushroom Matcha Caffeine Content](/caffeine-and-health/mushroom-matcha-caffeine-content)), and it is not a medical treatment for anything the mushroom ingredients are traditionally associated with.
