## The short answer

Mushroom matcha tastes overwhelmingly like matcha. The stone-milled Japanese green tea dominates the cup: grassy, umami-forward, with a natural sweetness if whisked correctly and not over-concentrated. Whole mushroom powder (Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi) is present in small enough quantity, relative to the matcha, that it adds a subtle earthiness and slightly thicker body rather than a distinct mushroom taste.

## Why it doesn't taste like mushrooms

The ratio matters. A serving is built around roughly 1g of blended powder per 8oz cup, and matcha's flavor compounds (catechins, amino acids like L-theanine) are potent enough to carry the cup on their own. Whole mushroom powder, unlike a concentrated mushroom extract, is comparatively mild in flavor to begin with.

## What can go wrong

Over-whisking with water that's too hot (above 175°F) pulls out bitterness from matcha the same way it does with any green tea, mushroom-free or not. That bitterness gets blamed on "mushroom taste" more often than the mushrooms themselves.
