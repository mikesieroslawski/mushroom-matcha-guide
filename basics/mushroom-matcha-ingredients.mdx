## The core ingredient list

A straightforward mushroom matcha blend contains: ceremonial-grade matcha (the tea base), Lion's Mane mushroom powder, Cordyceps mushroom powder, and Reishi mushroom powder. That's the full list on a clean-label product, no fillers, no added sugar, no flavoring needed since matcha itself carries the flavor.

## What each ingredient contributes

| Ingredient | Role |
|---|---|
| Ceremonial matcha | Base flavor, caffeine, L-theanine, catechins |
| Lion's Mane | Traditionally associated with focus and mental clarity |
| Cordyceps | Traditionally associated with stamina and energy |
| Reishi | Traditionally associated with calm and stress balance |

## Red flags on a lower-quality label

- Rice flour, maltodextrin, or other bulking starches listed
- "Mushroom blend" with no individual mushrooms named
- Matcha grade not specified (ceremonial vs culinary)
- Artificial flavoring, since real matcha doesn't need it
