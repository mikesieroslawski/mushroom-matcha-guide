## What good color looks like

Ceremonial-grade matcha should be a vivid, almost neon green, a direct result of shade-growing (which boosts chlorophyll) and careful stone-milling that avoids heat damage. A dull, olive, or brownish tint usually signals older leaf, sun-grown tea, or oxidation from poor storage.

## Does mushroom powder change the color?

Whole mushroom powder is typically a muted tan or brown. In small quantities relative to the matcha (roughly 1g of blended powder per serving, with matcha making up most of that), the mushroom content can slightly mute matcha's vividness compared to plain matcha, without turning the cup brown.

## Texture: what smooth vs gritty means

A fine, consistent stone-milled grind whisks into a smooth suspension with light foam. A coarse grind, whether from the tea or from unevenly milled mushroom powder, leaves gritty sediment at the bottom of the cup even after thorough whisking.
